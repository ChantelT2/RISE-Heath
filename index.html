Macros: type your own, or look up real data

The 🔍 button on each food item looks up real nutrition data — USDA FoodData Central first, then Open Food Facts (free, no key, carries some South African branded products) — and fills in the macros for the grams you enter. Get a free FDC key at fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-key-signup for higher lookup limits; without one it uses USDA's shared demo key (30 lookups/hour).
